Being referred to Kidscope

Children are referred into Kidscope by local Public Health Nurses. Referrals are made on behalf of children who live within the clinic catchment area.

How a referral is made

Children are referred into Kidscope by local Public Health Nurses located in St Mary’s Primary Care Centre, Baker’s Road, Gurranabraher, Cork. The clinic is held in Niche, Harbourview Road, Knocknaheeny, Cork, T23 XY16.

Walk in appointments are not accepted. Only families with a scheduled appointment will be seen on the day. If you think your child may benefit from Kidscope, please speak to your Public Health Nurse.

Catchment area

Kidscope operates in the area of Knocknaheeny on the North side of Cork City. Referrals are therefore made on behalf on children who are within the clinic’s catchment area.
