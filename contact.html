Contact us

The Kidscope clinic is based in the NICHE community centre on Harbourview Road in Knocknaheeny.

How to reach us

Clinic queries

Queries regarding the clinic can be directed to the Clinic Co-ordinator Ciara.

086 801 2360

Where the clinic is held

Harbourview Road

Calling in person

Any walk in queries can be left with the NICHE community centre administrator who will then pass on your query to the clinic co-ordinator.

Please note

Walk-in appointments are not accepted, and only families with a scheduled appointment will be seen on the day. If you think your child may benefit from Kidscope, please speak to your Public Health Nurse.
