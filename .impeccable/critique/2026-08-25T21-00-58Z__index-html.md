## Priority Issues

**[P1] The "2x2" help-grid doesn't render 2x2 at common desktop widths - this round's fix undermines its own intent.**
- Why it matters: .help-grid uses minmax(360px, 1fr) inside a 1120px-max .wrap. At any viewport >=1160px (1280/1366/1440/1920 - most desktop monitors), three 360px tracks fit (1116.8px) but four don't, so it renders 3 cards in row one, 1 orphaned card alone in row two - exactly the unbalanced layout the code comment says it was written to avoid. It only actually achieves 2x2 in a narrow ~1024-1157px band.
- Fix: Force an explicit repeat(2, 1fr) at a min-width breakpoint instead of relying on auto-fit/minmax arithmetic.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P1] The homepage's "Please note" callout omits the one actionable next step referrals.html's identical box includes.**
- Why it matters: The style is now consistent everywhere, but the copy isn't - only referrals.html adds "If you think your child may benefit from Kidscope, please speak to your Public Health Nurse." The homepage is the page most likely to catch a parent who doesn't have a referral yet, and it's the version that says "no" without saying what to do about it.
- Fix: Add the same PHN-redirect sentence to the homepage (and contact.html) callouts.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] The new persistent nav bar may wrap to two lines right at the breakpoint that introduces it.** (Code-estimated, not rendered.)
- Why it matters: The six links plus padding estimate to ~950-970px wide, but .wrap at a 960px viewport is only 920px - so the bar likely wraps to a centered two-line block exactly at its own on-ramp, before settling to one line above ~1000-1024px.
- Fix: Raise the breakpoint to ~1040-1080px or tighten link padding/gap; verify in a real browser through that range.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] Card-grid links expose their entire body copy as the accessible name (pre-existing, sitewide).**
- Why it matters: Each .link-card wraps heading + paragraph + "go" text in one <a>, so a screen reader announces the full sentence as the link name for all four cards - on the page's single most important decision point - while the partner-tile links two sections down get a deliberately concise name. Inconsistent care within the same page.
- Fix: Move the description text outside the anchor, or shorten the accessible name via aria-label.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P3] Hero opening sentence is a 42-word, jargon-dense run-on (pre-existing).**
- Why it matters: It's the literal first thing a stressed, first-time parent reads, and it works against the site's own plain-language/dyslexia-friendly intent elsewhere.
- Fix: Split into two shorter sentences; lead with what the clinic does for the child, not its governance structure.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify
